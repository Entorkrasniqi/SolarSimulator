# Mathematical Concepts in PV Panel Energy Calculations  

This document outlines the mathematical formulas and concepts used in the `Calculator.java` implementation for PV (Photovoltaic) panel energy calculations. The material from `Base.md` has been referenced where applicable to provide context.  

---  

## 1. Adjusted PV Panel Irradiance  

### Formula:  
\[ \text{Adjusted Irradiance} = \text{Solar Irradiance} \times (1 - \text{Cloud Cover}) \]  

### Explanation:  
This formula accounts for the reduction in solar irradiance due to cloud cover. The cloud cover is expressed as a fraction (0 to 1), where 1 represents full cloud cover.  

---  

## 2. Temperature-Adjusted Efficiency  

### Formula:  
\[ \text{Temperature Efficiency} = \text{Rated Efficiency} \times \left( 1 - \text{Temperature Coefficient} \times (\text{Current Temperature} - \text{Reference Temperature}) \right) \]  

### Explanation:  
This formula adjusts the efficiency of a PV panel based on the temperature difference from a reference temperature. The temperature coefficient represents the efficiency loss per degree Celsius.  

---  

## 3. Adjusted Temperature with Wind Cooling  

### Formula:  
\[ \text{Adjusted Temperature} = \text{Current Temperature} - (\text{Wind Speed} \times \text{Cooling Factor}) \]  

### Explanation:  
Wind cooling reduces the temperature of the PV panel, improving its efficiency. The cooling factor represents the temperature reduction per unit of wind speed.  

---  

## 4. Angle-Adjusted Irradiance  

### Formula:  
\[ \text{Angle-Adjusted Irradiance} = \text{Adjusted Irradiance} \times \cos(\text{Angle of Incidence}) \]  

### Explanation:  
The angle of incidence affects the effective irradiance received by the PV panel. The cosine of the angle (converted to radians) is used to calculate the adjustment.  

---  

## 5. Total Power Output  

### Formula:  
\[ \text{Power Output} = \text{Angle-Adjusted Irradiance} \times \text{Panel Area} \times \text{Panel Efficiency} \times \text{Temperature Efficiency} \]  

### Explanation:  
This formula calculates the total power output of a PV panel by combining the adjusted irradiance, panel area, and efficiencies.  

---  

## 6. Context from `Base.md`  

The `Base.md` document provides foundational knowledge about PV energy systems, including:  
- **Solar Irradiance**: The power per unit area received from the Sun.  
- **Temperature Effects**: The impact of temperature on PV panel efficiency.  
- **Angle of Incidence**: The angle between the sunlight and the panel surface, affecting energy absorption.  
- **System Losses**: Factors such as dirt, wiring, and inverter inefficiencies that reduce overall output.  

These concepts align with the calculations implemented in `Calculator.java`.

## 7. Example Calculation  

Using the formulas above, the `runCalc` method in `Calculator.java` demonstrates a sample calculation with the following inputs:  

## Weather inputs  
- **Solar Irradiance**: 800 W/m²  
- **Cloud Cover**: 30%  
- **Current Temperature**: 35°C  
- **Wind Speed**: 5 m/s  

## Panel type effects panel efficiency and temperature coefficient (= worse panel cools faster)  
- **Panel Efficiency**: 20%  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: 0.005 per °C  

- **Panel Area**: 1.5 m²  
- **Angle of Incidence**: 30°  

## Mounting position free-standing vs building-integrated affects cooling factor of panel  
- **Reference Temperature**: 25°C  
- **Cooling Factor**: 0.1°C per m/s  

The output is the total power generated by the PV panel, calculated step-by-step using the formulas above.  

## Example PV Panel Types  

### 1. Crystalline Silicon (c-Si)  
- **Typical Efficiency Range**: 17-22%  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: -0.35% to -0.45% per °C  
- **Reference Temperature**: 25°C  
- **Spectrum Response**: Good response to visible light, less effective in low-light conditions  
- **Degradation Rate**: ~0.5% per year  

### 2. Thin-Film CIS/CIGS (Copper Indium Selenide/Copper Indium Gallium Selenide)  
- **Typical Efficiency Range**: 13-16%  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: -0.30% to -0.35% per °C (better temperature performance)  
- **Reference Temperature**: 25°C  
- **Spectrum Response**: Better performance in diffuse light and high temperatures  
- **Degradation Rate**: ~1.0% per year initially, stabilizing after the first year  

### 3. Cadmium Telluride (CdTe)  
- **Typical Efficiency Range**: 14-18%  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: -0.25% to -0.32% per °C (good temperature performance)  
- **Reference Temperature**: 25°C  
- **Spectrum Response**: Good performance under high temperatures  
- **Degradation Rate**: ~0.6% per year  

### 4. Amorphous Silicon (a-Si)  
- **Typical Efficiency Range**: 6-9%  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: -0.20% to -0.25% per °C (excellent temperature performance)  
- **Reference Temperature**: 25°C  
- **Spectrum Response**: Better in low light and diffuse conditions  
- **Degradation Rate**: Higher initial degradation (light-induced), ~1.5-2.0% in the first year, then stabilizes  

### 5. Bifacial Crystalline Silicon  
- **Typical Efficiency Range**: 17-22% (front side)  
- **Bifaciality Factor**: 0.65-0.90  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: Similar to standard c-Si  
- **Special Consideration**: Gains from rear-side irradiance (typically 5-30% additional energy)  

### 6. Heterojunction Technology (HJT)  
- **Typical Efficiency Range**: 20-24%  
- **Temperature Coefficient**: -0.25% to -0.30% per °C (better than standard c-Si)  
- **Reference Temperature**: 25°C  
- **Degradation Rate**: ~0.4% per year
